About Me

Quality infrastructure specialist turning QA instincts into AI governance frameworks for enterprise agentic operations.

I'm a Senior QA software tester in the Salesforce ecosystem who learned the platform in 2023 and is now building expertise in AI agent orchestration and governance. My approach: weaponize a QA tester's instinct for "what breaks" to build better oversight for autonomous agents.

I focus on a specific gap that most frameworks miss: who is accountable when an AI agent makes a bad call? I'm building systems around RACI assignments, approval chains, and clear escalation paths for enterprise AI agents.

Beyond code: I'm married, building NBS Gym (a fencing & boxing coaching facility in Lexington), and plotting a path to financial freedom. Chicken Tights Labs is my cheeky umbrella brand — my mom thought that's what was for dinner.

AI Agent Governance Dashboard

A live demo of enterprise AI agent governance. Browse the agent registry, explore the ownership matrix, register new agents, read governance policies, and run governance checks that log to the audit trail.

Registered AI agents in the governance system. Each agent has a designated owner and RACI assignment responsible for its behavior, approvals, and ongoing review.

Ownership and responsibility matrix showing who owns which agents, approval chains, and review schedules.

Approval Chain: Owner → IT Security → Legal → CTO

Escalation Path: Owner → Manager → IT Security → CTO → Board

Review Schedule: Quarterly for Low/Medium risk · Monthly for High/Critical risk

Validates ownership, RACI assignment, approval chain, review status, and monitoring against 9 governance policies. Results are added to the audit trail.

Register a new AI agent to add it to the governance system. The system automatically generates approval chains, RACI assignments, and review schedules based on risk level.

Governance policies that define how AI agents are owned, approved, monitored, and reviewed. Nine policies covering everything from RACI assignments to incident response. Click any policy to read the full documentation.

History of all governance actions performed. Data persists in your browser's local storage.
